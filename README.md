# Should_Eniac_sell_on_Magist

## Project Overview
This project evaluates whether Eniac should partner with the Brazilian e-commerce platform Magist to enter the local market. Using transaction data from April 2017 to March 2018, our team analyzed pricing models, delivery logistics, and customer satisfaction tracking.

## Key Findings
* **Commercial Mismatch:** The median tech product price on Magist is just €65, with 75% of items costing under €150. Eniac's average product price of €540 exists entirely outside the current platform demand.
* **Logistics Volatility:** Delivery delays spike drastically during peak seasons, climbing to 16% late over Black Friday and hitting a peak of 20% late during Carnival closures.
* **The Reputation Penalty:** On-time deliveries average 4.2 stars, but late orders plummet to a 2.2-star average, with 56% resulting in devastating 1-star reviews.

## Final Recommendation
 We recommend declining the Magist contract .
